Site Plan

Site Name & Reason

Site Name: Art & Photography Showcase

Reason / Purpose Summary: To build an engaging, accessible, and responsive platform that displays curated visual artwork and photography while practicing dynamic web development techniques like data fetching, modals, and user preference persistence.

Site Purpose

The site provides curated artwork and photography to visitors, allowing them to browse pieces, learn about the artists and background, and explore featured/exhibition content. It delivers information visually and interactively through dynamic gallery rendering, accessible modals, and personalized display preferences.

User Scenarios

  • 1. A visitor wants to browse a collection of artworks to get inspiration for their own creative projects.
  • 2. A visitor wants to learn more about a specific artist and the story behind a piece.
  • 3. A visitor wants to see upcoming exhibitions or featured works on the home page.

Color Scheme & Usage

--color-bg (Dark charcoal)
Used for: headers, backgrounds, contrast panels.
--color-accent1 (Warm beige)
Used for: highlights, badges, button backgrounds.
--color-accent2 (Muted rose)
Used for: interactive elements, labels, wireframe accents.

Text colors adapt: light text on dark backgrounds and dark text on light areas to maintain readability and contrast.
