Our History

Central Service was founded in the early 1900s as a trade crossroads. Over the decades it evolved into a vibrant hub for small businesses, artisans, and regional commerce. The chamber has played a pivotal role in supporting growth and preserving local heritage.

Demographics

  • Population: ~85,000
  • Median Age: 34
  • Top Industries: Retail, Agriculture, Tech Services
  • Diversity Index: High (multi-ethnic community)
  • Education: 78% with secondary education or higher

Current Events

Summer Business Expo: Showcasing local innovators—July 12th at Central Plaza.

Community Market: Every Saturday, artisan vendors and food stalls.

Chamber Networking Breakfast: First Tuesday of each month.
